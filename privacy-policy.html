Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

No-logs policy. AMBER VPN does not monitor, log, or store your browsing activity, connection history, traffic content, or destination sites while the VPN is active.

What this policy covers

This policy explains what information AMBER VPN ("the app," "we," "us") collects, why, and how it's handled when you install and use the app. By using AMBER VPN, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we do not collect

We do not log or store:

  • Websites you visit or apps you use while connected
  • DNS queries or browsing history
  • Your original IP address alongside your VPN session activity
  • Traffic content, downloads, or data passed through the tunnel

Information we do collect

Some information is necessary to run the app, keep it stable, and support ads that fund the free service:

Data Purpose
Advertising ID Shown ads through Google AdMob, personalized where permitted by your device settings
Device info (model, OS version, crash logs) Diagnosing crashes and improving app stability
App usage events (e.g. connect/disconnect, session length) Aggregate performance monitoring — not tied to your browsing activity
Push notification token Delivering app notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging

Third-party services

AMBER VPN uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — serves in-app ads and may collect advertising identifiers
  • Google Firebase — crash reporting, push notifications, and app analytics

These providers may collect data independently under their own privacy policies. We don't control, and aren't responsible for, their data practices.

Permissions the app requests

AMBER VPN requests device permissions (such as VPN service access and network state) strictly to establish and maintain your VPN connection. These permissions are not used to inspect or log your traffic.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information described above is only shared with the third-party service providers listed here, as needed for the app to function (ads, crash reporting, notifications).

Data retention

Aggregate, non-identifying app performance data may be retained for as long as needed to maintain and improve the app. Since we don't log browsing or connection activity, there's no such data to retain or delete.

Children's privacy

AMBER VPN is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

Your choices

  • Reset or limit your device's advertising ID in your device's system settings
  • Uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection
  • Contact us to ask what data (if any) we hold about your device

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
